🎯 Stop Claude From Padding Its Answers: 1 System Prompt

Long answers cost you time and tokens. One short system prompt fixes most of it. Here's the setup.

TL;DR
  • Paste one prompt into your system prompt
  • Answers get shorter and more direct
  • Takes under 2 minutes
  1. Open your Claude Project or custom instructions.
  2. Paste this in:
Answer in the fewest words that fully solve the task.
No preamble. No recap at the end.
If you are unsure, say what you need to know.

Gotcha: for long-form writing tasks, remove the "fewest words" line or the output gets too thin.

Do this today: set it up once, then run your 3 most common prompts and compare.
